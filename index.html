Frequently asked questions

How does the Heist Payout Splitter calculate leader vs crew takes?
The tool starts with your total gross take from primary and secondary targets, subtracts bag damage losses, and deducts fencing and NPC crew cuts (typically 10-15%). The remaining net pot is divided according to each player's percentage. If "Reimburse leader's setup cost" is selected, the host is paid back their prep fee from the gross pot first.
What is considered a "Fair Split" in GTA heists?
With 4 players, an equal split is 25% each. However, since the leader pays the upfront setup fee and spends 40+ minutes completing solo preps, the established community standard is 40% for the leader and 20% for each crew member. For 2 players, 50/50 is fair if preps were shared, or 60/40 if the host prepped alone.
Which GTA 6 heist gives the highest money per hour ($/hr)?
Based on historical data from the Cayo Perico and Casino heists, solo-capable heists with fast infiltration routes (like the Port Gellhorn Depository) yield the highest money per hour (~$2.8M-$3.2M/hr) because there is no requirement to wait for teammates or split the primary vault loot.
Can you solo major GTA 6 heists?
Yes, several activities in the calculator are labeled "Solo Ready". When playing solo, duffel bag space limits how many secondary items (gold, cash, narcotics) you can carry, but you retain 100% of the net payout without paying player cuts.
How do I share my calculation with my heist crew?
Click the "Copy breakdown" button at the top to copy a clean, formatted Discord breakdown with bullet points, or click "Share link" to get a URL with all your current cuts, fees, and numbers encoded for instant loading.
Why are numbers marked as PLACEHOLDER?
GTA 6 Online numbers in this tool are based on community leaks, trailer details, and historical GTA Online baselines. All values are stored in an open activities.json file and will be updated with day-one verified numbers upon official launch. You can click "Report number" to suggest corrections.
How does the Goal Tracker calculate time to afford an item?
Enter your target vehicle or property price and your current bank balance. The tracker calculates remaining funds needed, rounds up to the exact number of activity runs required (using Math.ceil), and shows the total grind hours and leftover surplus cash.
